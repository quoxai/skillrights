Registry directory

Opt-in listing of publicly registered skills from the SkillRights Registry, an append-only transparency log at registry.skillrights.org. Registration is free and needs no account. Unlisted registrations are counted in the totals below and never listed here, though the log itself is public in both modes.

A listing here is evidence, not a verdict: it establishes that an exact artefact existed by a recorded time, and records the key and signature submitted with it. A signature is shown as verified only where the registry could check it against the registered hash; otherwise it is shown as submitted. Nothing here proves legal ownership, authorship or originality, and the registry does not adjudicate disputes.

Register a skill#

From the skill directory, with the CLI (0.2.3 or later):

npx skillrights register            # unlisted: only the hash and evidence leave your machine
npx skillrights register --public   # adds name and description to this directory

Both modes return a permanent SRID and save a portable receipt (Merkle inclusion proof plus signed tree head) that is verified locally before it is written, and re-checkable offline forever with npx skillrights receipt. In unlisted mode the registration is real and provable but appears here only in the aggregate count, until you choose otherwise. Unlisted is not confidential: what you submit (hash, claimed author, key, signature, licence and mode) enters the public append-only log either way, and unlisted controls only this directory listing.

What it proves#

A registration proves three things: that this exact artefact (by SHA-256 hash) existed by the recorded time; that a particular key and signature were submitted with it, and whether that signature verified against the registered hash; and the order of registrations relative to each other. A verified signature relates signed bytes to a key. It does not relate that key to a person or organisation, which is separate evidence the registry does not hold. Version lineage (supersedes) is a claim, recorded verbatim, never certified. Nothing here proves legal ownership.

Verify it yourself#

The log is designed to be checked without trusting us: mirror the entries, recompute the Merkle root, and verify the external anchors. Every tree head is countersigned by an independent RFC 3161 time-stamping authority and submitted to public OpenTimestamps calendar servers, which batch it into the Bitcoin blockchain; a fresh root reads as pending for a few hours until that confirmation lands, and the per-root state is always live at /api/v1/log/anchors (and in the evidence line at the top of this page). The recipe, with exact commands, is in MIRRORING.md.
