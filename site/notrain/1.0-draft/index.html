"Execute" means to read, load, interpret, follow or otherwise use the

organisation's behalf, to perform tasks, including loading the Work into

the Work at inference time.

"Train" means to use the Work, in whole or in part, in the training,
pre-training, fine-tuning, alignment, evaluation-for-improvement or other
improvement of a machine-learning model; to distil a model or capability
from it; to generate synthetic training data derived from it; or to
include it in any dataset, embedding corpus or index assembled for any of
those purposes.

"Redistribute" means to copy, publish, host, mirror or convey the Work to
third parties, in original or modified form.
