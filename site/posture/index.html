Legal context#

  • EU: DSM Directive Art 4(3) machine-readable TDM rights reservation; EU AI Act Art 53(1)(c) (in force 2025-08-02) requires GPAI providers to honour such reservations including for training outside the EU. Which formats qualify is pending European Commission guidance (consultation closed 2026-01-09). (verified 2026-09-10)
  • US: Bartz v. Anthropic (N.D. Cal. 2025) held training on lawfully acquired books to be fair use; the enforceability of a machine-readable reservation against a lawful acquirer was not adjudicated. Settlement final approval 2026-07-20. (verified 2026-09-10)
